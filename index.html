About

关于我

I am an Associate Researcher and Ph.D. Supervisor at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU), working closely with Professor Guangtao Xue. My research is conducted within the Shanghai Key Laboratory of Web3 Trusted Data Circulation and Governance, and I am pleased to contribute as a member of the CCF Internet of Things Special Committee.

我是上海交通大学计算机学院助理研究员、博士生导师，与薛广涛教授紧密合作。我的研究依托上海市 Web3 可信数据流通与治理重点实验室开展，并担任中国计算机学会（CCF）物联网专委会委员。

My research interests lie at the intersection of the Internet of Things (IoT), Intelligent Sensing, and Large Language Models (LLMs). I am driven by the goal of bridging the gap between physical sensing and intelligent cognitive understanding to create more intuitive and secure systems. Recent topics include electromagnetic and acoustic side channels, RF radiance-field modeling with 3D Gaussian Splatting, and multimodal LLMs for hardware design.

我的研究兴趣位于物联网（IoT）、智能感知与大语言模型（LLM）的交叉点，致力于弥合物理感知与智能认知之间的鸿沟，构建更直观、更安全的系统。近期课题包括电磁与声学侧信道、基于 3D 高斯泼溅的射频辐射场建模，以及面向硬件设计的多模态大模型。

I feel fortunate to work alongside a team of exceptionally talented colleagues and students whose insights constantly inspire my work. Through these collaborative efforts, I have had the opportunity to share our findings in venues such as USENIX Security, NSDI, ACM MobiCom, ICML, ACM IMWUT/UbiComp, IEEE INFOCOM, and IEEE TMC. I was also deeply honored to receive the 2025 Shanghai Computer Society Science and Technology Award (First Prize). Together, our students also built 巫萌 (MagicGenesis), the group's own AI-agent research platform.

很幸运能与一群才华出众的同事和同学并肩工作，他们的想法时常给我启发。我们的成果发表在 USENIX Security、NSDI、ACM MobiCom、ICML、ACM IMWUT/UbiComp、IEEE INFOCOM 和 IEEE TMC 等会议与期刊上，我也很荣幸获得2025 年度上海市计算机学会科学技术奖一等奖。同学们还共同打造了课题组自己的 AI 智能体科研平台巫萌。

Research

研究方向

Six connected threads, from how signals physically leak to how machines understand them.

六条相互关联的研究主线：从信号如何在物理上泄漏，到机器如何理解它们。

Core motivation核心动机

Most sensing systems work in the lab but fail in the "wild." My goal is to sense the unreachable and the invisible. Whether it is an ultra-weak signal under the ocean, a hidden device in a secure room, or a fragmented data pattern in a power grid—I focus on the physics of the bottom layer to build systems that are robust, deployable, and industrially meaningful.

许多感知系统在实验室里表现出色，到了真实环境却会失效。我的目标是感知难以触及、肉眼不可见的事物：无论是海洋深处的超弱信号、保密房间里的隐藏设备，还是电网中碎片化的数据模式，我都从底层物理出发，构建鲁棒、可部署、有产业价值的系统。
