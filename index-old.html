Biography

I am an Associate Researcher supervised by Professor Guangtao Xue at SJTU. My research primarily focuses on the Internet of Things (IoT), Intelligent Sensing, and Large Language Models.

I have published over 20 papers in top-tier conferences and journals including USENIX SECURITY, NSDI, MOBICOM, and TMC. I am also the recipient of the 2025 Shanghai Computer Society Science and Technology Award (First Prize).
