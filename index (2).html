Biography

I am an Associate Researcher supervised by Prof. Guangtao Xue at SJTU, and co-founder of the Cross-Research Center for Digital Twin Simulation and Intelligent Connected Sensing. My research focuses on Internet of Things (IoT), Intelligent Sensing, and Large Language Models.

I have published over 20 papers in top-tier venues including USENIX Security, NSDI, MobiCom, and IEEE TMC. I am a recipient of the 2025 Shanghai Computer Society Science & Technology Award (First Prize).

Research Demos & Systems

SingAttack — Acoustic Side-Channel Attack on Speech Recognition

Covert attack exploiting high-frequency sounds from power supplies to inject malicious commands into speech recognition systems — entirely contactless, no physical access required. Demonstrated on commercial smart speakers and smartphones.

USENIX Security 2023Side-Channel

SingSPirit — Appliance Interaction via Power Supply Acoustics

Captures faint acoustic signatures from power supplies via deep learning, enabling e-bike battery monitoring, anomaly detection, and device state inference without any invasive sensors. Successor to SingMonitor & SingAttack.

SenSys 2026

MagPrint / MagPrint++ — Electromagnetic User Fingerprinting

Continuous fingerprinting framework exploiting unintended EM emanations from built-in mobile magnetometers. Enables high-accuracy user behavior fingerprinting and privacy auditing with zero specialized hardware. Evolved from INFOCOM 2020 to IEEE TMC 2026.

IEEE TMC 2026INFOCOM 2020

Anti-Surveillance Detection System

Industry-deployed detection system co-developed with Huawei Data Communications achieving 99% accuracy at detecting covert surveillance devices — the industry's first production-grade solution of its kind.
